The Market Inefficiencies 02 / 09

1. Financial Friction & Spreads

International tourists lose up to 15% of their travel budget to banking spreads, foreign exchange fees, ATM surcharges, and credit card FX markups.

2. Severe Language Barrier

Direct negotiation with independent local guides, artisans, and family-owned businesses is impossible due to language barriers, locking out micro-vendors.

3. OTA Exploitation & Fragmentation

Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) extract 15%–30% predatortial commissions from local vendors while forcing tourists to juggle multiple fragmented apps.
